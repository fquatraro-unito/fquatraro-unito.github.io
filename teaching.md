## Teaching and programmes

- **Co-Director**, International MSc Programme in Economics of Innovation for Sustainable Development, University of Torino and Université Côte d’Azur (since 2024).
- **Vice-Director**, PhD Programme in Innovation for the Circular Economy, University of Torino, after directing the programme from 2017 to 2024.

### Courses at the University of Torino

- Economics of Innovation (taught in English)
- Digital Technologies and the Green Economy
- Management of Innovation
- Innovation for the Circular Economy (PhD course, taught in English)
- Economic Policy

Earlier courses at Torino include Economics of the Digital Era, Economics of ICTs, Green Economy and Innovation (taught in English), Global Economy and Multinational Corporations (taught in English), Labour Economics and Economics.

### Courses at the Université de Nice Sophia Antipolis (2010–2014)

Time-series and panel-data econometrics, microeconomics and data analysis (Master 1); regional and spatial economics and innovation systems and territories (Licence 3 and Master 1); empirical analysis of regional dynamics and innovation and economic geography (Master 2).

## Current roles

- **Director**, ICxT Innovation Center, University of Torino: an interdepartmental applied-research centre connecting university research with firms, public institutions and territorial actors.
- **Co-Editor**, *Spatial Economic Analysis* (since 2018) and *Italian Economic Journal* (since 2022).
- **Coordinator**, Spoke 5 – Innovation Ecosystems for the Circular Economy in GRINS (PNRR).
- **Fellow**, Collegio Carlo Alberto and Global Labor Organization; **external affiliate**, GREDEG, CNRS and Université Côte d’Azur.

## Research collaboration

I contribute to national and European research initiatives on circular economy, innovation ecosystems, green technologies, labour-market dynamics and regional transformation. My work also includes scientific coordination of PRIN projects and policy-oriented collaboration with Centro Economia Digitale and the European Commission Joint Research Centre.
