## Research

My research lies at the intersection of economics of innovation, green and circular economy, economic geography, industrial dynamics, knowledge production and labour-market change. I study how technologies, skills and knowledge networks shape firm performance, regional development and sustainable transitions, using applied econometrics and policy-evaluation methods.

### Research strands

- **Circular economy, green innovation and sustainability transitions:** circular economy technologies, green investments, eco-innovation, environmental policy, sustainable development and digital tools for circular transformation.
- **Regional knowledge dynamics, economic geography and innovation ecosystems:** regional diversification, knowledge flows, innovation networks, smart specialisation, technological branching and the geography of innovation.
- **Firms, labour markets, entrepreneurship and technological change:** firm growth, entrepreneurship, corporate governance, labour-market dynamics, wages, skills, ICTs, knowledge bases and the microeconomics of innovation.

## Research leadership and collaboration

I coordinate and contribute to national and European research initiatives on circular economy, innovation ecosystems, green technologies, labour-market dynamics and regional transformation.

Recent roles include Coordinator of Spoke 5 – Innovation Ecosystems for the Circular Economy in GRINS (PNRR), scientific coordination of PRIN projects on circular economy innovation and innovation/labour-market dynamics, and policy-oriented work with Centro Economia Digitale and the European Commission Joint Research Centre.

I direct the [ICxT Innovation Center](https://www.icxt.unito.it/) at the University of Torino, an interdepartmental applied-research centre connecting university research with firms, public institutions and territorial actors.
