## Curriculum vitae

Francesco Quatraro is Full Professor of Economics at the University of Torino and Fellow at the Collegio Carlo Alberto. He is co-editor of *Spatial Economic Analysis* and the *Italian Economic Journal*. At the University of Torino he directs the ICxT Innovation Center and is Vice-Director of the PhD Programme in Innovation for the Circular Economy. His research focuses on the economic determinants and impacts of technological innovation at the firm and regional level, with a specific focus on the interplay between new technological challenges, sustainable development and labour-market dynamics. He is an applied economist with extensive experience in econometric analysis of firms’ and regions’ economic and innovative performance and in counterfactual evaluation of policies. He has served as an individual expert on strategic technological challenges for the EU Joint Research Centre and non-profit organisations.
