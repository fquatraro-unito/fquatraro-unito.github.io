## Research projects

I coordinate and take part in national and European research projects on circular economy, green technologies, innovation ecosystems and labour-market dynamics. This page lists current and past projects, competitive grants and policy-oriented consultancy.

## Current projects

- **Green finance and vocational training for corporate compliance** (2026–2028). Scientific coordinator.
- **GRINS – Growing, Resilient, INclusive and Sustainable** (2023–2026). Extended Partnership funded by the Italian National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR). Coordinator of Spoke 5 – Innovation Ecosystems for the Circular Economy. [grins.it](https://www.grins.it/)
- **VisitPNRR – The PNRR in the twin and circular transition: ex-ante evaluation and ex-post indicators** (2025–2026). Scientific coordinator.

## Past projects

- **Circular Economy Innovation and the socio-economic impacts on sectors and regions** (2023–2025). Research Project of National Interest, PRIN 2022 PNRR. Scientific coordinator.
- **Innovation and labour market dynamics** (2023–2025). Research Project of National Interest, PRIN 2022. Local principal investigator.
- **Innovation and global challenges in an interconnected world: the role of local resources and socio-economic conditions** (2020–2023). Research Project of National Interest, PRIN 2017. Scientific coordinator.
- **CLIP – Coopération pour l’Innovation aPpliquée** (2018–2023). EU INTERREG ALCOTRA programme (PITEM). Local unit coordinator.
- **proGIreg – Productive Green Infrastructure for post-industrial urban regeneration** (2018–2022). EU Horizon 2020. Nature-based solutions for urban regeneration with and for citizens.
- **WWWforEurope – Welfare, Wealth and Work for Europe** (2012–2016). EU 7th Framework Programme, grant 290647. Milestone leader; final output: *Regulatory push-pull effects on innovation: an evaluation of the effects of the REACH regulation on patents in the chemical sector* (WWWforEurope Working Paper No. 91).
- **Gouvernance d’entreprise et innovation** (2012–2017). CNRS-GREDEG. Co-director.
- **PICK-ME – Policy Incentives for the Creation of Knowledge: Methods and Evidence** (2011–2014). EU 7th Framework Programme, grant 266959. Deputy coordinator.
- **IPER – Incentive Policy for European Research** (2010–2014). Collegio Carlo Alberto.
- **Knowledge Intensive Sectors: Models and Evidence** (2009–2010). French National Research Agency (ANR).
- **EURODITE – Regional Trajectories to the Knowledge Economy: A Dynamic Model** (2005–2010). EU Integrated Project.
- **DIME – Dynamics of Institutions and Markets in Europe** (2005–2010). Member of the EU network of excellence.
- **The Economics of Structural Change in Knowledge** (2010). International Centre for Economic Research (ICER).
- Earlier applied research for CSI Piemonte (economics of network services in Piedmont, 2007), MIUR (technological performance in the Italian North-West, 2006), Fondazione Rosselli (knowledge production in Piedmont and Valle d’Aosta, 2005) and the EU project TELL on technological knowledge and localised learning (2003).
